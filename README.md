# EgeStore

EgeStore Android / Google Play release workspace.

This repository contains the EgeStore release-preparation toolset. It does **not** submit an app to Google Play automatically: Google Play Console authentication, developer account setup, app signing, policy declarations, and final review remain actions performed by the app owner in Play Console.

## Contents

- `play-store-checklist.md`: release requirements and console steps.
- `store-listing.md`: draft Play Store listing.
- `privacy-policy-template.md`: privacy-policy drafting checklist (must be completed to match actual data handling).
- `android/README.md`: Android packaging instructions.
- `web/`: installable storefront prototype and local product editor.

## Current status

This is a release-preparation scaffold, not a live store or published app. The included browser storefront stores data locally in the current browser only. It does not accept payments, synchronize products, deliver customer orders to the owner, or connect to couriers. Do not advertise it as a live e-commerce service until those services are implemented and tested.

## Trademark and product authenticity

EgeStore is an independent store concept and is not affiliated with Apple. Do not list concept models, handmade mock-ups, or unavailable products as genuine commercial devices. Only publish goods that you actually own or are authorized to sell, with accurate descriptions, prices, stock, shipping, and return terms.
