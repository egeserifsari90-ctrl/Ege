# EgeStore — draft Play Store listing

**App name:** EgeStore

**Short description (draft):** Discover products and manage your EgeStore orders.

**Full description (draft):**

EgeStore is an independent shopping experience for browsing products offered by the EgeStore shop.

Planned features:
- Browse product listings with photos and prices.
- Search the catalog.
- View product details and stock availability.
- Manage shopping basket and checkout.
- Follow order status and contact the shop.
